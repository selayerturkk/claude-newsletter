Feature Deep-Dive

Mastering Dynamic Workflows in Claude Code

The biggest productivity unlock in Claude Code isn't a model upgrade—it's dynamic workflowsAI that creates and runs multi-step processes automatically. This feature lets Claude orchestrate complex, multi-agent tasks that would normally require careful human coordination.

What it does: Dynamic workflows are orchestration scripts Claude writes for your task and runs across many subagents in the background. Use them when a task is too large for one conversation to coordinate.

How to use it:

  1. Enable ultracodeClaude Code's highest effort setting for complex tasks mode in Claude Code's effort menu
  2. Describe a complex, multi-step task (like "audit our entire codebase for security vulnerabilities")
  3. Claude will automatically decide if a workflow is needed and create one
  4. Monitor progress with the `/workflows` command
  5. Review the consolidated results when complete

Best use cases: Codebase-wide bug hunts, profiler-guided optimization, security audits, large migrations, and framework modernization efforts spanning thousands of files.

Why it matters: Instead of you breaking down a massive task into smaller pieces and coordinating them manually, Claude handles the entire orchestration. It's like having a senior developer who can manage a complex project end-to-end while you focus on higher-level decisions.

Enterprise note: Dynamic workflows are off by default on Enterprise plans—admins can enable them in Claude Code settings.

The AI Policy & Competition Landscape

Trump's AI Executive Order Shifts Government Stance
President Trump issued an executive order seeking to give the U.S. government up to 30 days to assess frontier AI models before companies release them. The order also asks companies to collaborate with the administration to "select trusted partners" for early model access.
Apple's Gemini-Powered Siri Surprise
Tim Cook announced a Gemini-powered Siri and multi-AI Extensions system that makes Claude an iPhone option for the first time during what reports suggest was his final WWDC keynote as Apple CEO.
The Great American AI Act Divides Stakeholders
Representatives Jay Obernolte and Lori Trahan released a 269-page bipartisan AI framework that would impose a three-year federal preemption of state AI laws. Labor unions rejected it as "a giveaway to the AI industry," while tech groups praised the unified national approach.
Google I/O Reveals Agent-First Search
Google announced "Search agents" that can be customized for different tasks and operate 24/7 to monitor for changes. AI Mode has surpassed one billion monthly users, with queries doubling each quarter.
Colorado's AI Law Goes Live in 25 Days
Colorado's Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence Act—the first comprehensive state AI law in the US—takes effect June 30, 2026, requiring developers of high-risk AI systems to protect residents from algorithmic discrimination.
The Bigger Picture

Racing Toward Autonomous Development

This week's news reveals a startling acceleration: AI systems are increasingly building themselves. Claude writing 80% of its own code isn't just a milestone—it's a warning flag about the speed of progress. For aspiring product managers, the question isn't whether AI will automate development work, but how quickly you can adapt your skills to manage and direct AI systems rather than just human teams.

The market is responding with remarkable speed. Three trillion-dollar AI companies are racing to go public this year, while governments scramble to establish oversight before capabilities outpace regulation. Meanwhile, Apple's embrace of multi-AI systems signals that the future isn't about one dominant AI assistant—it's about orchestrating multiple specialized AIs for different tasks.

The strategic takeaway for emerging PMs: focus on learning how to evaluate, direct, and coordinate AI capabilities rather than trying to outcompete them on pure execution speed. The companies that win will be those that best combine human judgment with AI automation.
